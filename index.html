WHO WE ARE

Established in 1987 in Varayaal, Mananthavady (Wayanad, Kerala), Parathottam Karshaka Vikasana Samithi (PKVS) is a registered Non-Governmental Organization dedicated to holistic community development and ecological stewardship.

Registered under Section XXI of the Indian Societies Registration Act, 1860, PKVS has worked for decades to strengthen rural communities while protecting and restoring the ecosystems on which they depend.

For over three decades, PKVS has operated at the intersection of modern agricultural innovation, social welfare, and environmental conservation, transforming rural livelihoods across the region.

Our ethos centers on bridging traditional rural wisdom with scientific advances to foster self-reliant, resilient, and inclusive communities.
